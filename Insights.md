\# Insights



\## Insight 1



Average game time of Bots are higher than real players. Ideally game time of bots should be significantly lower compared to real gamers. Assuming the ability to kill are lower in bots, these numbers mean the bots are placed in regions that are away from interactions with real players.



**GrandRift is an extreme case with this. (Stat in the BI visualisations)**



\### Value and Recommendation/ Inference

Bots should be placed for confrontation with players early in the game to promote player confidence. Real players get easy kills early in the game and gain confidence moving into the game.



----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



\## Insight 2



Loots are distributed admirably well in Lockdown but with the other two maps, the heat map reveals there is a huge concentration of loots towards the center of the map. This means it is implied to move towards the middle of the map regardless of where you start. This makes the game a little more linear.





\### Value and Recommendation/ Inference



Whether the  design team wants to keep this to maintain ease of directional thinking for gamers or wants to change the center of action with each game using algorithms depends on the firm's choice on **gamer psychology**. Use of bots becomes of significance once again.



----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



\## Insight 3



The time distribution of loots and combats show high number of events at initial gaming minutes as expected, but there are consistent spells of inactivity after this in GrandRift and Lockdown. This implies there are spells in the game where the game is only movement for players, making it less engaging. 



Looting towards the end of the game is low as well, but there is a possibility this could be intentional so not deriving anything from it.





\### Value and Recommendation/ Inference

**The stat reveals parts of the game that could be monotonous.** Increase possibility of looting an killing in areas of the Lockdown and GrandRift with possibility of movement in these frameworks.



----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------



\## Insight 4



GrandRift needs significant redistribution with resources. This is the most questionable map off the three. Despite having significant gamer and bots across the map, especially in corners, looting events are significantly concentrated in the middle of the map and sparse on a lot of other places more than the other two maps



This indicates a significant number of players needing to navigate without significant loot for most of the game



**or**



The early spikes in time distribution of loot indicates high number of gamers starting from thee middle of the map.





\### Value and Recommendation/ Inference

Peripheral space should be used better for events in GrandRift. Needs better Looting and kill distribution as well.
